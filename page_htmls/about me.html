Georgie Ubben

Junior Strategist

People are always looking for something worth believing in.

It’s why we get out of bed in the morning. It’s also why we’ll spend $8 on a coffee when there’s a perfectly good pot at home. It’s not all math. We’re products of believing.

At Duke, I studied economics and religion, which sounds like a peculiar pairing, but it turns out they’re both about value. Where it comes from, who decides it, and why people will give up so much to hold onto it. Most of our days revolve around what will make it better. Chasing an answer to make everything else make sense.

That’s how I see advertising too. People are searching, and half the time they don’t even know what for. Advertising seeks to give them something not only worth choosing but something worth believing in. Because when the idea is right, and the words convey a valuable conviction, it can become something much bigger than the product.

I help make those kinds of connections happen. And hopefully leave a few things better than I found them.
